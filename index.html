    About this app

    Text comes from "BDE Standards Book as of 11 AUG" (52 pages), in the book's order, with its own spelling and grammar. Pages 45–51 (the two EO memos) are scanned images read by OCR, with obvious scan errors fixed. Photos, insignia and charts are the book's own images. The Army Song lyrics (p. 11) aren't reproduced. Quiz questions are written from the book and from the policy updates listed under Rule Changes. Where the book contradicts itself, the quiz uses the stricter rule. Ask your chain of command when in doubt.

    Unofficial study aid built by SPC Ortiz David. It isn't an official 44th Medical Brigade publication. The official Dragon Medic Standards Book, Army regulations and the chain of command govern. The update notes and Red Pen items are offered respectfully as recommendations for the book's next edition.
